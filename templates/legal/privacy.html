Privacy Policy Last updated: {% now "F j, Y" %}

Marbaras (“we”, “us”, or “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how you can control it.

Consent

By using our website (www.marbaras.com), you consent to this Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.

Information we collect

The personal information requested will be clear at the point of collection. If you contact us directly, we may receive additional details like your name, email, phone number, message contents, and attachments.

When you register for an account or place an order, we may collect details such as name, company, address, email, telephone number, and order information.

How we use your information

  • Process and fulfill your orders;
  • Provide, operate, and maintain our website;
  • Improve, personalize, and expand our services;
  • Understand and analyze how you use the site;
  • Develop new products, services, features, and functionality;
  • Communicate with you for support, updates, and marketing;
  • Send transactional/service emails;
  • Detect, prevent, and address fraud or abuse.

Log files

We use standard server logs (IP address, browser type, ISP, timestamp, referring/exit pages, clicks). These are not linked to personally identifiable information and are used for analytics, site administration, and aggregated statistics.

Cookies & web beacons

We use cookies to store preferences and improve your experience. You can control cookies in your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some features.

Advertising partners

Third-party ad networks may use cookies, JavaScript, or web beacons to personalize ads and measure performance. They receive your IP address automatically when serving ads. We do not control third-party cookies—please review their privacy policies.

Third-party privacy

Our policy does not apply to other websites or advertisers. Consult their policies for details, including opt-out instructions. You can also manage cookies via your browser settings.

CCPA privacy rights

  • Disclosure: Request categories and specific pieces of personal data we have collected.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data (subject to exceptions).
  • Opt-out of sale: Request that we do not sell your personal data.

To exercise these rights, contact us as described below. We aim to respond within one month.

GDPR data protection rights

  • Access: Request copies of your personal data.
  • Rectification: Request corrections or completion.
  • Erasure: Request deletion in certain circumstances.
  • Restriction: Request limits on processing.
  • Objection: Object to processing.
  • Portability: Request transfer of your data to you or another organization.
We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling requests. Our target response time is one month.

Children’s information

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided such information, please contact us so we can promptly remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new policy on this page, and changes take effect immediately upon posting.
