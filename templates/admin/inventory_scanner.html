📦 Inventory Scanner

Scan products to view, add, or remove stock

How to use:

  • Scan a barcode using your scanner (or type manually)
  • The system will find the product by: Serial Number, SKU, or Product ID
  • View Stock: Shows current stock without changing it
  • Add Stock: Increases stock by 1
  • Remove Stock: Decreases stock by 1
  • If product has variants, scan the variant SKU to manage variant stock
